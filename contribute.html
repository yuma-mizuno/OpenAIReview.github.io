Review your own paper

Follow the mathematics review instructions and open the findings alongside your PDF.

1. Review the manuscript

Give your coding agent the review instructions, the complete TeX source, its matching PDF and a private work folder outside this repository. Manuscript content is processed by your agent's model provider under its terms.

2. Open the PDF review

Follow the setup guide to save the review JSON, source snapshot and catalog beside the viewer.

python -m http.server 8080 --bind 127.0.0.1 --directory /path/to/private-review

Open http://127.0.0.1:8080/?paper=paper&contributed=1. Check every highlighted passage against the PDF.

3. Share with your readers

Deploy the folder with access for the intended audience. It contains the PDF, TeX source and review.
